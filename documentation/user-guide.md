# User Guide

1. Open DermaScan AI and create an account with a unique email and password of
   at least eight characters.
2. Complete the self-reported skin profile. Enter only allergies or sensitivities
   you already know; the questionnaire is not a clinical assessment.
3. Prepare one recent front-facing image with even light, neutral expression,
   no filters, minimal makeup, and glasses removed when practical.
4. Upload a JPG, JPEG, or PNG up to 5 MB and explicitly accept temporary image
   processing. Do not include another person.
5. Review image quality. Failed images must be replaced; warnings require an
   explicit choice before continuing.
6. Review face detection. The system checks only for one usable face and does
   not recognize identity.
7. Review the broad skin-type estimate. Low confidence or questionnaire conflict
   remains `Uncertain`.
8. Review visible observations. These labels describe image appearance, not
   acne, rosacea, allergy, infection, or another diagnosis.
9. Review eligible catalogue products, exclusions, score explanations, freshness,
   and limitations. The score is relevance, not safety or effectiveness.
10. Review the generated morning and night routine and introduce products
    gradually according to current labels.
11. Generate the final report, print it, or create a temporary PDF. Privacy mode
    controls how much self-reported detail appears in the PDF.
12. Optionally submit feedback or product experience. Consent for analytics and
    research review is separate, and feedback can be withdrawn.

Users can edit the skin profile, delete a temporary upload, archive reports,
withdraw feedback, remove product avoidance, and log out. Logout removes local
authentication state but does not server-revoke an already issued stateless JWT.

DermaScan AI provides general skincare guidance based on visible facial
characteristics and user-provided information. It is not a medical diagnostic
system, does not prescribe treatment, and does not replace advice from a
qualified dermatologist.

Users experiencing severe, painful, infected, persistent, rapidly changing, or
unusual skin concerns should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
